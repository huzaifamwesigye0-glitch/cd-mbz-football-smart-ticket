How V5 calculates its numbers
It uses bookmaker-market consensus from the returned odds. For each match it converts prices to implied probabilities, removes the bookmaker overround, compares the result across books, checks bookmaker agreement and the best available price, and calculates an estimated price value. V5.1 also applies a quality gate and a maximum ticket length so the odds target cannot force weak selections. These are market estimates, not independently verified win probabilities or guarantees.
Important: This system cannot guarantee winning bets. V5 can return NO BET instead of forcing weak selections. Keep a record of every prediction, including losses, if you use this as a public prediction service.
